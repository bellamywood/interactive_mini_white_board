Classroom-friendly tips
  • Pen draws. Eraser removes ink. Text places a draggable/resizable text box (drag by the top bar).
  • Lock board freezes everything except Save PNG and Flip (useful when you want pupils to stop editing).
  • Flip gives a clean back side without losing the front.
  • Save PNG downloads a picture of the current side. Open PNG loads a saved picture onto the current side (then you can draw on top).
Shortcuts (when not typing): P=Pen, E=Eraser, T=Text, L=Lock board, F=Flip, Ctrl/Cmd+Z=Undo, Ctrl/Cmd+Y=Redo
